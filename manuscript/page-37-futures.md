## Five possible futures (research edition)

Plausible research trajectories for 2026–2050 ▲ — stated with their uncertainty, not their hype.

:::chips
:::chip 1 · Exoplanet atmosphere census ▲
:::chip 2 · Gravitational-wave astronomy matures ▲
:::chip 3 · Dark universe surveys answer back ▲
:::chip 4 · Quantum sensing infrastructure ▲
:::chip 5 · Sustainable space operations ▲
:::

| # | Trajectory | Driver | What "success" looks like in 2050 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Exoplanet atmosphere census** ▲ | JWST-class spectroscopy [R01, R02] | dozens of rocky worlds with spectra; 1–2 contested biosignature candidates ◆ |
| 2 | **Gravitational-wave astronomy mature** ▲ | detector networks [R17] | thousands of events/yr; continuous-wave detections; multi-messenger routine |
| 3 | **Dark universe survey answers** ▲ | Euclid + Rubin [R11, R12] | dark energy's constancy tested at percent level; Hubble tension resolved or sharpened |
| 4 | **Quantum sensing infrastructure** ▲ | clocks & interferometry [R13] | space-based atom interferometry flown; VLBI sharpened by optical clocks |
| 5 | **Sustainable space operations** ▲ | debris pressure [R14] | debris removal routine; traffic management binding; lunar logistics demonstrated |

:::pull
Every row is an instrument-and-method future — not a Universe future ::est::.
:::

**What is NOT on this list, on purpose:** first contact, FTL travel, terraforming, uploaded minds, or a "theory of everything". None currently has a credible research path ::est::.

**Advanced corner.** Failure modes for each row are knowable: budget attrition [R21], launch delays, and the classic — a null result nobody wanted (which is still a result ::est::).
