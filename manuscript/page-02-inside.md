## Inside the project

:::cards
:::card The mission
ASTRO-NOT is an independent science-magazine experiment: one bold question — *Will the Universe change drastically by 2050?* — examined honestly across 48 pages. We argue the answer for the Universe itself is **almost certainly no** — ::est:: — while our *understanding* of the Universe, and humanity's presence in it, could change substantially. That distinction drives everything between these covers.
:::
:::card How to read the labels
Every claim carries one of four confidence labels, each with its own icon and colour, defined once and used consistently (see the chips below). Learn four icons and you can audit every page of this magazine — and every headline you read outside it.
:::
:::

:::chips
:::chip ● Established science
:::chip ◆ Active research
:::chip ▲ Plausible projection
:::chip ✦ Fictional scenario
:::

:::callout The simulation policy
Any chart or dataset marked **SIMULATED** is synthetic, generated with a fixed random seed so it can be rebuilt and checked. It illustrates methods, never evidence. Simulated figures carry axis labels, units and uncertainties — because a plot without units is a rumour with axes.
:::

:::cards
:::card What we promise
Every claim tagged. Every simulation labelled. Every source on p 47. When we don't know, we write **[NEEDS VERIFICATION]** instead of guessing.
:::
:::card What we ask
Read with suspicion and curiosity in equal measure — then make your p 8 predictions, including about us.
:::
:::
