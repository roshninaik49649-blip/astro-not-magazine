## Life beyond Earth

**Intro.** If there's life on another world, how would we know without going? The leading plan: **biosignatures** — combinations of gases in a planet's atmosphere that are hard to explain without biology ::est:: (oxygen + methane coexisting is the textbook example).

**Going deeper — the false-positive problem.** Non-living processes can mimic biology ◆:

- Water vapour oceans around small stars can slowly photo-chemically build oxygen without life
- Volcanism and photochemistry can produce gases classed as biosignatures
- A "habitable-zone" orbit around an active small star may not be habitable at all ◆

So one spectrum ≠ one conclusion. Researchers now design tests that look for *combinations* and *context* — chemistry, surface, star type, repeated observations ◆ [R02].

**Technosignatures.** A parallel search looks for technology: narrow-band radio signals, laser pulses, industrial pollutants. Programmes like Breakthrough Listen scan millions of stars' worth of data [R19] ◆. So far: intriguing anomalies, no confirmed signal ::est::.

**Advanced corner.** Bayesian framing helps: the probability that a detected gas is biological depends on the prior that life exists at all — which we cannot yet measure from a sample size of one habitable biosphere. Expect any 2050-era "life candidate" to be probabilistic and contested ◆, not a verdict.

**Caption.** The honest headline we'd print if a candidate appears: *"Something interesting. Not yet life."*
