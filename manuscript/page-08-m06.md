## Mission M06 — Your prediction log

Make five predictions about 2026–2050, log their confidence, and state what evidence would **falsify** each one. Open the predictions archive:

### QR code → missions/m06-predictions/index.html

**The five prompts:**

1. How many exoplanets will be confirmed by 2050? (Anchor: the current count is public [R01] — look it up first.)
2. Will a biosignature candidate be announced? Which confidence label would you give it?
3. Will the Hubble tension be resolved — dissolved, confirmed, or still contested?
4. How many people will have travelled to space by 2050?
5. Our own bet: *the Universe will not change drastically by 2050.* Agree or disagree — and say what would change your mind.

**How it works.** Each prediction gets a confidence slider (from "just a hunch" to "would bet a year's salary") and a falsification prompt: *what observation would prove me wrong?* If you can't name one, that's a warning sign — predictions that can't be tested aren't really predictions.

**Privacy note, stated in the app itself:** everything stays in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded; clearing your browser data clears your log.
