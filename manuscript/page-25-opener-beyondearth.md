# CHAPTER 04
## Beyond Earth

The Universe won't change by 2050. Our address book might. This chapter is about humans becoming a two-world species — the science, the engineering, the economics and the honest limits.

The Moon is back on the manifest: NASA's Artemis programme aims to return crews, with international partners building around it [R10] ::est::/▲. Mars remains the destination that headlines chase — and this magazine measures hype against radiation doses, logistics and biology (p 27) ::est::.

But living off-Earth is mostly unglamorous: power budgets, water loops, shielding, repair schedules (p 28). We look at what a permanent foothold would really take — and at India's remarkable space programme, which separates its verified achievements from its ambitions better than most (p 29).
