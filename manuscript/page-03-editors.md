## From the editors

**The question.** You've probably seen the headlines: "By 2050 we'll have... " followed by a dazzling claim about warp drives, terraformed Mars, or a new picture of reality. It's tempting to treat the Universe the same way we treat phones: every cycle must bring a revolution.

:::pull
The Universe doesn't run on upgrade cycles. The laws of physics have not changed measurably in billions of years.
:::

The Planck 2018 results — the most precise cosmic census ever taken — describe a cosmos whose large-scale behaviour is stable on human timescales [R05] ::est::. Stars burn for millions of years. Galaxies collide over hundreds of millions. Nothing "drastic" happens on a 24-year clock ::est::.

**What *can* change by 2050** is us: our instruments, our missions, our models, our data pipelines — and our skill at telling the difference between a breakthrough and a mirage. That's the real story of this magazine.
