## Five claims to question

Popular claims you'll meet before 2050 — each audited against evidence. Keep this page; you'll need it.

:::cards
:::card 1 · "Terraforming Mars is achievable within decades" ✦
Reality: atmosphere engineering on that scale exceeds current total industrial capacity by orders of magnitude ::est:: [R16]; no credible plan exists. Habitats ≠ planets.
:::
:::card 2 · "Mars colonisation solves Earth's problems"
Reality: radiation doses near career limits ::est:: [R15]; resupply dependence for decades ▲. Emigration is not currently a mass option — Earth remains the only planet with free air.
:::
:::card 3 · "Physics is nearly complete"
Reality: quantum mechanics and general relativity both pass every test we can construct ::est:: — and remain mutually incompatible at the deepest level ◆. "Nearly complete" was claimed in 1894; the century since disagrees.
:::
:::card 4 · "AI will discover new physics by itself"
Reality: machines shortlist; humans validate ::est:: (p 34). No confirmed physics discovery has been made by an unsupervised model ◆.
:::
:::

:::callout 5 · Our own claim: "the Universe will not change drastically by 2050"
We put ourselves on the list ::est::. Cosmic change operates on billion-year scales; instruments and knowledge change on 24-year ones ▲. You know our answer — and p 8 asks you to falsify it.
:::

:::pull
The pattern in all five: a true ingredient ::est:: inflated into a guarantee ▲. Question inflation, not ambition.
:::
