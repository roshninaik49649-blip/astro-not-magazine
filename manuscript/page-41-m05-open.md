**Mission M05 — the case of observation AN-2050-017** ✦

A fictional case file, assembled to train one skill: weighing evidence like a scientist ✦.

**The setup ✦.** Late in the 2040s, a fictional survey telescope logs observation **AN-2050-017**: a transit-depth spectrum of a rocky exoplanet catalogued as TOI-7150 b (a fictional designation ✦). Night 1 shows an intriguing dip pattern. Then night 7 — observed by a *different* instrument in the fictional story — shows an extra feature near 4.6 µm that night 1 lacked.

Three rival explanations are on the board ✦:

:::chip H3 · Instrumental
:::

Your job across the case pages (pp 42–44) is to weigh them — spectra, logs, timestamps and all ✦. The classified rationale unlocks on page 44 and via the QR portal.
