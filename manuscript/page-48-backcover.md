**The Universe: no.** Its laws are stable, its timescales are measured in billions of years, and 24 of those years is less than a blink ::est::.

**Our picture of it: entirely.** Every instrument we fly, every survey we run, every tension we chase between now and 2050 redraws the map — the map, not the territory ::est::.

Keep your predictions. Mark your confidence labels. Argue with the page that's wrong. The Universe isn't going anywhere. We are.
