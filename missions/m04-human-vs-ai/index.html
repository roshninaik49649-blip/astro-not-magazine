The debrief pattern

The simulated classifier over-trusts sharp, point-like shapes (cosmic-ray hits and spikes) and occasionally rejects genuinely irregular variability. Machines bring coverage and consistency; humans bring context and the “wait, that's odd” moment.

What the artefact kinds look like
  • Cosmic-ray hit: one sharp pixel/epoch only.
  • Bad column: vertical streak, always same pixel column.
  • Diffraction spike: streaks through a bright star at fixed angles.
  • Saturated bleed: flat bright square around a bright source.
