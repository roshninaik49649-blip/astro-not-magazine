Why multiple detectors matter

The first detection (GW150914) was confirmed because two independent detectors saw the same waveform milliseconds apart. One detector alone cannot tell a chirp from a glitch. The matched filter used here — correlating the stream against a template — is the real method, simplified.

False-alarm thinking
